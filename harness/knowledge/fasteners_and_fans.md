# Fasteners, inserts and fans (design notes)

Metric clearance holes (medium fit): M2 2.4 mm, M2.5 2.9 mm, M3 3.4 mm, M4 4.5 mm, M5 5.5 mm.
Tap-drill sizes for threading plastic directly: M2 1.6, M2.5 2.05, M3 2.5, M4 3.3 mm.
Heat-set insert holes (typical brass inserts): M2 3.2 mm, M2.5 3.5 mm, M3 4.0 mm, M4 5.6 mm;
boss outer diameter at least insert diameter + 3 mm.
Counterbore for socket-head screws: M3 head 5.5 mm dia x 3 mm; M4 7 mm x 4 mm. Countersink
90 degrees for flat-head screws.
Hex nut pockets: M3 5.5 mm across flats (model 5.7), 2.4 mm thick; M4 7 mm (model 7.2), 3.2 mm.

Axial fans (frame size -> screw hole spacing, hole diameter, common depth):
40 mm -> 32 mm, 3.5 mm, 10 mm; 50 mm -> 40 mm, 4.3 mm, 10 mm; 60 mm -> 50 mm, 4.3 mm, 15 mm;
70 mm -> 61.5 mm, 4.3 mm, 15 mm; 80 mm -> 71.5 mm, 4.3 mm, 25 mm; 92 mm -> 82.5 mm, 4.3 mm, 25 mm;
120 mm -> 105 mm, 4.3 mm, 25 mm; 140 mm -> 124.5 mm, 4.3 mm, 25 mm.
Fan opening: frame size minus 4 mm as a round or rounded-square opening; add a finger guard
grid for openings over 60 mm. Duct to a narrower outlet: keep the taper under 30 degrees total
and the outlet area at least 60 percent of the fan area; a 20-30 mm straight section before the
fan reduces noise.
Blower/centrifugal fans exhaust tangentially; the duct must wrap the outlet, not the intake.
