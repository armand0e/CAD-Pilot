# General mechanical design notes

Material thickness for printed brackets: 3-4 mm; add 45-degree gussets under cantilevers.
Snap fits (PLA/PETG): cantilever 1.2-1.6 mm thick, 8-12 mm long, 0.8-1.2 mm catch; PETG or
ABS flex better than PLA. Living hinges: not reliable in PLA.
Press fits: bearings and magnets 0.05-0.1 mm interference; loose fits 0.2-0.3 mm clearance.
Chamfer the leading edge (0.5 mm) of any press-fit or lid lip so it starts straight.
Round bar/shaft holes for rotation: shaft + 0.3-0.5 mm.
Keep-out: allow 8-10 mm above a Raspberry Pi GPIO header for a ribbon connector; 3 mm above
a bare header.
Text and logos: emboss 0.6-1 mm high or deboss 0.6 mm deep; letter height 6 mm or more for
FDM legibility; sans-serif fonts.
Draft is not needed for FDM; it is needed (1-2 degrees) for silicone moulds or injection.
Art and organic forms: build from lofts between 3-6 sections, pipes along paths for handles
and stems, revolves for vases/bowls (wall 1.2-2 mm for vase mode), and fillets on outer edges.
